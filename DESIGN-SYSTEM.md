## 3. LUXURY DESIGN LANGUAGE RULES

Since the brand color palette is frozen, the entire $50,000 visual transformation is created through:

### A. Typography & Scale
- **Display Serif**: `Playfair Display` (weights: 400, 500, 600, 700). Used for collection titles, product titles, hero headlines, editorial quotations, and high-impact price typography.
- **Body & Metadata Sans**: `Jost` (weights: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700). Clean geometric proportions with letter spacing `tracking-wider` (0.05em to 0.15em) for uppercase luxury micro-labels and badges.
- **Admin System Sans**: `Poppins` & `Jost` for dense, highly legible data grids and analytics.

### B. Layout & Whitespace
- **Asymmetric Editorial Grids**: 12-column responsive layout with generous vertical rhythm (`py-16` to `py-24` on desktop).
- **Breathing Space**: Minimum `gap-8` on product grids, preventing cramped catalog aesthetics.
- **Floating Cart Drawer & Header**: Glassmorphism with locked `--glass-light` (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.92)`) ensuring content scrolls smoothly underneath without visual clutter.

### C. Photography & Aspect Ratios
- **Portrait Ratio**: Strict `3:4` or `4:5` (`aspect-portrait`, `aspect-editorial`) for all garments.
- **Hover Micro-Zoom**: Subtle scale pop (`transform: scale(1.04)`) with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` 600ms transition.

### D. Component Design Standards
- **Buttons**:
  - Primary: `#103D42` background, `#DFC3A0` text, subtle shadow, rounded-xl/full.
  - Outline: `1px solid rgba(16, 61, 66, 0.25)`, `#103D42` text, hover background `rgba(16, 61, 66, 0.04)`.
  - Accent: `#DFC3A0` background, `#0A2A2E` text.
- **Badges**:
  - Delicate pill badges with uppercase `tracking-[0.15em] text-[10px]` using `#103D42` and `#DFC3A0` borders.
